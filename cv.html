Research Positions

Sept. 2022 — present

Doctoral Student — Vachon-Presseau Lab, McGill University

Continuing work on sex and gender effects in pain while expanding into neuroimaging, with a current focus on layer fMRI and the integration of expectation, learning, and sensory input. Coursework includes Machine Learning for Biomedical Data and Advanced MR Imaging and Spectroscopy of the Brain.

Sept. 2020 — Aug. 2022

Master's Student — Vachon-Presseau Lab, McGill University

Studied sex differences in chronic pain from a population neuroscience perspective, quantifying the relative contributions of psychosocial gender and sex hormones using UK Biobank and Northern Finland Birth Cohort data (combined N > 500,000). Identified female gender norms as a chronic pain risk factor and uncovered a sex-by-testosterone effect in women mediated by inflammation. Trained in graph theory, high-performance computing, and neuroimaging analysis at Neuromatch Academy and through McGill graduate coursework.

May 2019 — Sept. 2020

Research Assistant — Moayedi Lab, University of Toronto

Ran study visits with adolescent participants using quantitative sensory testing, MRI, and self-report to investigate the effect of stress on neuroanatomy and pain persistence. Spearheaded a pilot study on endocannabinoids and pain in collaboration with researchers in Ireland; this work formed the basis of my honours thesis.

May 2019 — May 2020

Research Assistant — Rizvi Lab, University of Toronto

Conducted study visits including questionnaires, interviews, neuroimaging, and computerized tasks investigating depression and suicidality. Contributed to a review article on reward processing and suicidality.
