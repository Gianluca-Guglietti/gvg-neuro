  • 28 January 2026 · Pain · Computational

    Control, predictability, and the precision of pain expectations

    Habermann and Büchel dissociate control from predictability in a thermal pain paradigm and show that control sharpens the precision of pain priors rather than shifting their mean. The result has implications for any paradigm in which the two factors are confounded.
